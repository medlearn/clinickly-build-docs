# Panel credential checks — clean labels

**The fix:** the label names the check; the *standard* (genuine, role-appropriate, proportionate, complete, role-scoped) moves to help text. Same principle as the policy field labels — a UI label should not carry the verifier's instruction.

Sentence case, no qualifier on the label. Help text is one line, shown under the label or on hover. "Renews" = carries an expiry date and prompts re-verification.

| # | Label | Help text (the standard behind it) | Renews |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Identity** | Photo ID checked against a recent photograph. | — |
| 2 | **Right to work** | Confirmed before starting; re-checked before expiry. | ✔ |
| 3 | **CV** | Full career history on file. | — |
| 4 | **Contract and terms** | Signed contract, including confidentiality and data-processing terms. | ✔ |
| 5 | **DBS check** | Criminal-records check at the level appropriate to the role. | ✔ |
| 6 | **Employment history** | Full history, with any gaps explained. | — |
| 7 | **References** | Two references, confirmed directly with the referees. | — |
| 8 | **Fitness to work** | Confirmed fit for the role, with any reasonable adjustments. Health detail is not held here. | ✔ |
| 9 | **Induction** | Completed before working independently. | ✔ |
| 10 | **Professional registration** | Current on the GMC / GPhC / NMC register. | ✔ |
| 11 | **Professional indemnity** | Insurance covering this role and this work. | ✔ |
| 12 | **Clinical qualifications** | Original certificates seen and verified. | — |
| 13 | **Competence** | Assessed as competent for this role and service. | ✔ |
| 14 | **Appraisal and revalidation** | Annual appraisal complete; revalidation on track. | ✔ |

## Why this reads better

- **"References"** not "two genuine references" — of course they're genuine; that's what *verified* means. The genuineness is the standard, not the name.
- **"DBS check"** not "role-appropriate DBS check" — the *level* is a detail of the check, not part of its name.
- **"Fitness to work"** not "proportionate fitness-to-work decision" — and the help text carries the data-minimising point (health detail not held here), which is the part that matters.
- **"Professional indemnity"** not "current role-scoped professional indemnity" — "current" is shown by the expiry date; "role-scoped" is the standard, in help text.

## Note on two near-duplicates

**CV (3)** and **Employment history (6)** overlap. Keep both only if they mean different things: CV = the document supplied; Employment history = the verified, gap-checked timeline. If they're the same evidence, merge into one **Employment history** check and drop CV.
